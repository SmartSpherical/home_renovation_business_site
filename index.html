How Heat Pumps Work

Winter Heating Mode

Even when it's cold outside, the refrigerant absorbs heat from the air and transfers it indoors—efficiently heating your home.

Summer Cooling Mode

The system reverses to pull heat from your home and release it outside, keeping you cool and comfortable all summer.

Why Choose Heat Pumps?

Heat pumps are an energy-efficient alternative to traditional furnaces and air conditioners. They move heat instead of generating it, which can dramatically reduce energy use — especially when paired with modern insulation and smart thermostats.

  • High seasonal efficiency (heating & cooling)
  • Lower operational costs and reduced carbon footprint
  • Flexible options: ductless minisplits to whole-home systems
